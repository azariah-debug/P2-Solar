P2 CleanTech Labs

Advancing carbon mitigation through science.

P2 CleanTech Labs is exploring biological approaches to carbon mitigation through scientific research, academic collaboration and government-supported innovation.

Current research stage

The organization is in the foundational research phase.

  • Scientific literature review
  • Technology assessment
  • Commercial opportunity evaluation
  • Intellectual property landscape review
  • Research planning

Academic partnership

The company is collaborating with a major Canadian university laboratory to evaluate carbon mitigation opportunities involving biological systems.

Government-supported innovation

Grants and innovation programs support the evaluation of climate-focused technologies that may contribute to future emissions reductions.

Areas of research interest

Biological carbon capture

Exploring biological mechanisms that naturally remove carbon from the atmosphere.

Microbial carbon utilization

Assessing ways biological systems may contribute to carbon reduction pathways.

Environmental biotechnology

Investigating technologies with potential environmental and climate applications.

Synthetic biology

Evaluating future opportunities involving engineered biological systems designed to improve carbon mitigation capabilities.

Research roadmap

The intended progression of the research. P2 CleanTech Labs is in the foundational phase; later stages are planned, not completed.
